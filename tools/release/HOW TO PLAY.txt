TempestRPG - playing together
=============================

Everyone: unzip and run TempestRPG.exe (no installation). Windows may warn about an unknown
app the first time: "More info" -> "Run anyway". Allow it through the firewall when asked.

Every world is single player until its owner switches on multiplayer. Your world runs on your
own computer: no server to rent, no ports to open, no IP addresses to type.

PLAYING TOGETHER (invite codes)
  Host:   in your world press Esc -> "MULTIPLAYER: OFF" (it switches on) -> "New invite code"
          -> "Copy code" and send the short code (TRPG-XXXXX-XXXXX) to ONE friend.
  Friend: TempestRPG.exe -> PLAY -> pick your character (or a new one) -> "Join a Friend" ->
          paste the code -> "Join". That's it - no reply code, no router settings.
  One code per friend (up to 8 players); a code works for 15 minutes.
  (If the game says the internet relay is unreachable, it shows a long code instead: then the
  friend gets a reply code to send back, and the host pastes it -> "Let them in".)
  Esc -> "MULTIPLAYER: ON" switches it off again: friends leave, their characters stay saved.
  Codes contain your Internet address: share them only with friends. Some strict networks
  (mobile hotspots, school/company networks) can block invite codes.

YOUR OWN CHARACTER IN A FRIEND'S WORLD
  The host decides: Esc -> "GUESTS: NEW CHARACTERS" / "GUESTS: OWN CHARACTERS OK".
  When it is allowed, pick one of your own characters when joining: your level, skills, gear,
  items, Zeni, professions, fish log, achievements and quests come along. It stays one
  character: what you gain at your friend's is written back into your own world (on every
  autosave and when you leave). The map you discovered stays with each world.
  Otherwise you play a new character in their world (saved in their world for next time).

STORAGE
  Build Vaults in your village (B, build mode) for settlement storage; open it at a Vault or at the
  town hall of any major city (the "Storage" sign). In a major city you can also rent storage that
  opens only in that city.

CLASSES AND ATTRIBUTES (new in 1.1)
  Every level gives 4 attribute points (Strength, Dexterity, Intelligence, Vitality): spend them
  with the + buttons in the equipment window (I -> Equipment).
  Once your settlement is founded, an old master moves in: talk to him (F) to choose a class -
  Warrior (swords), Assassin (daggers), Lancer (spears), Reaper (scythes), Archer (bows), Mage
  (staffs: a free magic bolt) or Monk (fist weapons: very fast jabs). Your class teaches two class
  skills (the second at level 25) and from level 20 you can add a subclass for a second weapon
  type. Every weapon needs its class; without a class you can use weapons up to Rare. Changing a
  class or resetting your attributes costs Zeni at the master.

GUILDS AND CHAT
  Press Enter to chat: plain text goes to everyone in the world, "/g text" to your guild,
  "/p text" to your party. P -> "Guild" founds a guild (1000 Zeni: a name, a short tag and an
  emblem) or shows yours: the leader and officers invite friends who are online.
  Build a Guild Hall in your village (B): F there opens your guild's bank (items, gear, Zeni),
  its level and perks, and this week's three guild quests that everyone helps with.

DUELS AND THE ARENA
  P -> "Duel" next to a friend challenges them (they answer in their Party window). Fight inside
  the ring: the blow that would finish someone wins instead - nobody dies, nothing is lost.
  Build an Arena in your village: matches there are best of three and go on the arena board (F).

YOUR HOUSE
  Build "Your House" in your village (B). Its door (F) leads inside; F inside buys furniture and
  places it where you stand. Rest in your bed for +10% XP (20 min); a trophy gives a little more
  Zeni, the wardrobe opens your storage, the stove lets you cook. Friends can visit.
  Or rent a room at a major city's town hall (F there -> "Rent an inn room").

CHARACTERS (new in 1.5)
  PLAY shows your characters with their stats and progress. Play one's world, take it to a
  friend's world (if they allow own characters), or make a new character (name and race).
  Names show above everyone's head (Options: "Show my name" hides your own).
  Options -> Keys: any action can go on a key or a mouse button; the HUD shows its icon.
  Salvage old gear into bars, cloth and gems at your Forge or in a town (equipment window).

TOOLS, SPRINT AND LOOT (new in 1.3)
  You start with a basic axe, pickaxe and fishing rod. F at a tree or rock chops or mines it;
  F facing a lake or the sea casts your line. Better tools: merchants sell the second tier, the
  rest you craft at your camp's Forge or in a town (Professions, L -> Woodcutting / Mining /
  Fishing): each needs that profession's level and logs, ores or fish of that level. Seven
  tiers - Basic, Bronze, Iron, Silver, Gold, Mithril, Adamantite (rods: Birch ... Jadewood).
  Every tree, rock and pebble can be chopped or mined; glowing mushrooms are picked (Glowcap).
  Every water can be fished: small ponds only hold small fish, lakes more, the sea everything -
  and something huge lives in Mirror Lake.
  Hold Shift to sprint (the bar under you is your stamina).
  Loot pops out of creatures and chests: walk over it, or press F close by, to take it.
  The log box (bottom left): - and + make it smaller or bigger; the mouse wheel scrolls back.

HANDY
  Your saves are in %APPDATA%\TempestRPG (type it into the Explorer address bar).
  Esc -> Options: HUD size (65-100%), window size, sound.  F1: hide/show the controls line.
