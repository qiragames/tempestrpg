TempestRPG - playing together
=============================

Everyone: unzip and run TempestRPG.exe (no installation). Windows may warn about an unknown
app the first time: "More info" -> "Run anyway". Allow it through the firewall when asked.

Every world is single player until its owner switches on multiplayer. Your world runs on your
own computer: no server to rent, no ports to open, no IP addresses to type.

PLAYING TOGETHER (invite codes)
  Host:   in your world press Esc -> "MULTIPLAYER: OFF" (it switches on) -> "New invite code"
          -> "Copy code" and send it to ONE friend (Discord, chat...).
  Friend: TempestRPG.exe -> "Join a Friend" -> paste the code -> choose a character -> "Join".
          The game shows a REPLY code -> copy it and send it back to the host.
  Host:   paste the reply code -> "Let them in". Esc -> "Invite a Friend" makes a code for the
          next friend (one code per friend, up to 8 players).
  Esc -> "MULTIPLAYER: ON" switches it off again: friends leave, their characters stay saved.
  Codes contain your Internet address: share them only with friends. Some strict networks
  (mobile hotspots, school/company networks) can block invite codes.

YOUR OWN CHARACTER IN A FRIEND'S WORLD
  The host decides: Esc -> "GUESTS: NEW CHARACTERS" / "GUESTS: OWN CHARACTERS OK".
  When it is allowed, pick one of your own characters when joining: your level, skills, gear,
  items, Zeni, professions, fish log, achievements and quests come along. It stays one
  character: what you gain at your friend's is written back into your own world (on every
  autosave and when you leave). The map you discovered stays with each world.
  Otherwise you play a new character in their world (saved in their world for next time).

STORAGE
  Build Vaults in your village (B, build mode) for settlement storage; open it at a Vault or at the
  town hall of any major city (the "Storage" sign). In a major city you can also rent storage that
  opens only in that city.

HANDY
  Your saves are in %APPDATA%\TempestRPG (type it into the Explorer address bar).
  Esc -> Options: HUD size (65-100%), window size, sound.  F1: hide/show the controls line.
